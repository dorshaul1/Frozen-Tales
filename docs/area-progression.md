# Area preparation and pacing

The existing open river remains traversable at every rod tier. Only authored ice sheets require the Icebreaker. `world/preparation.ts` contains advisory rods and contrasting three-slot builds; advice never gates fishing or movement.

- Starting River: easy, reliable catches and short selling trips. Basic Rod; 65% Whitefish. Save for Reinforced Rod.
- Blue Ice Bend: Pike runs and short currents. Reinforced Rod; storage / brace / stabilizer fishing build.
- Frozen Lake: deep-water trout and longer fights. Arctic Rod; finder for locating activity. Storage / brace / canopy suits open-water fishing.
- Glacier Gorge: high-value Dolly Varden/Lenok, tight passages and strong flow. Expedition Rod advised; motor / stabilizer / hull sacrifices cargo and fishing brace for navigation.
- Caves: specialized fish and darkness, layered over regional difficulty. Personal Lantern is separate from the three slots; Icebreaker / motor / storage emphasizes access and range.

Balance changes: lake pool 15 Salmon / 30 Pike / 55 Trout. No fish prices changed. Existing base-value weighted means rise from $6.9 to $20.8 to $30.5 to $43.5 before weather, bait, habitats, market and specimen variation. These are not guaranteed payouts.

Reinforced/Arctic/Expedition rods remain $60/$180/$420. A modeled five-fish starter route funds the first rod after two successful trips, with no quest/market bonus. Later rod purchases take roughly two successful trips in the corresponding test pools; navigation, missed fish and optional gear purchases add time. Finder now costs $110. Rod Mount control is .07/.12. Stabilizer stroke bonuses are .08/.16/.24, retaining steering/current improvements while Turbo remains the burst-speed choice.

No save fields changed, and purchased ownership is retained. Existing cave/rare/Trophy rules remain intact. River Chart advice is only attached to discovered areas, the Journal recommends rods by fish difficulty, and entry notices give brief equipment advice.

Checks: `/checks/area-progression.html` exercises fresh-save catches/seller income, purchases, real fight parameters, build tradeoffs and save/load. `/checks/cave-fight.html` covers actual cast/fight/land integration and all species' fight models. These controlled checks do not replace subjective long-form playtesting of every possible loadout.
